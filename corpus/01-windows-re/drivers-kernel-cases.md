### The Age-Old Art of SSDT Hooking (But Bypass Most ARK Tools) (orig: The Age-Old Art of SSDT Hooking(But Bypass Most ARK Tools))
(wowelf) **Hiding tables in SSDT slots**: the service descriptor table has 4 slots (ntoskrnl/win32k/NotUse1/NotUse2) and ARKs check only the first two; copy both tables into the NotUse slots (same for the Shadow tables) and patch the `inc large dword ptr fs:638h` inside `KiSystemService` (the syscall counter — a useless instruction) into `add edi,0x20` (cHackCode=`83 C7 20`+nop padding; edi originally points at the ntoskrnl/win32k descriptor, +0x20 lands exactly in a NotUse slot) — only one equal-length 6-byte instruction changed, no long jump written; R.K.U/IceSword both miss it. Afterwards modify the copied tables freely — the originals stay byte-identical. Wrap the table edits with `cli/mov eax,cr0/and eax,not 1000h/mov cr0,eax` to clear WP, restore after.
